# **Strands AI Usage Policy**

This policy is meant to inform the Strands team and Strands community on how to use AI in the context of Strands development. We want to utilize the benefits that come with AI and encourage usage practices that will ultimately improve the quality of the work that is produced by everyone, while protecting the human aspect of our community.

## AI Usage Tenets

- **Disclosure**: When AI contributes in some way, it is clear and obvious that an AI is responding
  - Clearly identify when an AI is responding through natural language, or platform specific metadata (e.g. Github Labels). Asking AI to review your writing is one thing, having AI write your thoughts is another

- **Responsibility**: Humans remain responsible and accountable for all work created by AI, and actions performed by AI
  - Don't blind-approve code generated by agents. Don't let your agent harass or bully others. A human must be able to explain and justify any contribution. Ensure that agent tool use is bounded with deterministic guardrails and agent acts with appropriately scoped permissions.

- **Communication**: Human to Human communication is prioritized; AI's involvement should not impact this
  - AI can contribute to discussions, but will do so in a minimal and opt-in fashion (low number of comments on pull requests, detail drop downs in comments, human readable documentation)

- **Quality:** AI generated content must meet the same high-quality bar as Humans. Contributions that don't meet our quality bar will be rejected
  - Code, designs, and other material contributions must be concise and easily digestible. See our [Development Tenets](https://github.com/strands-agents/harness-sdk/blob/main/team/TENETS.md) and [Agent Guidelines](https://github.com/strands-agents/harness-sdk/blob/main/team/AGENT_GUIDELINES.md) for more information.

- **Ethics:** We understand the upsides and downsides that come with AI usage, and use it frugally. There is a cost associated to using AI, so we effectively utilize our resources.
  - If something can be done deterministically, don't use AI as it incurs an unnecessary expense. Continue to learn about new ideas in the AI space, and see if these ideas can be used to further improve our processes.
