At Skai, our customers rely on Studio's agents to make changes in live ad accounts across retail media, search and social. Strands gave us the ability to keep humans in control. Every agent step in Studio runs on Strands in AgentCore Runtime. Using hooks and interrupts ensure an agent stops and waits for approval before it touches a campaign, without forking the agent loop.
